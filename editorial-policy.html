This Appendix is a model document referred to in Clauses 1.5, 1.8 and 5.2.4 of the moderAItor Master Service Agreement. It is binding on the Client only to the extent the Client has adopted it as its own moderation policy.

About this document

The Model Comment Moderation Editorial Policy is a template that media organizations and civil society organizations can adopt, in full or with amendments, as their own public editorial policy for moderating comments on their social media pages. It describes what kinds of comments are not allowed, how flagged comments are reviewed, how temporary hiding and final decisions work, and how users can raise objections.

Under Clause 5.2.4 of the Master Service Agreement, each Client must develop, define and apply a publicly available, transparent and lawful moderation policy, and may use this model policy as a basis.

Publication

The text of the model policy is being finalized and will be published on this page in Armenian and English. Once published, it will carry its own version number and effective date, and previous versions will remain available at permanent links.
